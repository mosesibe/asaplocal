01Problem

Both sides of this market distrust the middle.

Local services is one of the last large consumer categories where the intermediary is the least-trusted party in the transaction. That distrust is earned, and it comes from two directions at once.

Homeowner's problem

They rarely know what the problem is called, which trade fixes it, or what it should cost — so every job starts with guesswork.

And a "verified" badge usually means a form was completed once, at sign-up, possibly years ago. Insurance certificates are collected as a PDF and never looked at again.

The homeowner carries a risk they were told was handled.

Tradesperson's problem

They buy introductions, not outcomes. A single job is resold to as many buyers as the platform can find, so the same work gets quoted into the ground.

Visibility goes to the biggest advertising budget and the longest review history, so a skilled newcomer can't get a foot in the door.

Bad leads — wrong area, wrong trade, no answer — are treated as the operator's cost, not the platform's.

These are the same problem seen from opposite ends: trust in this category is asserted rather than proven. Whoever closes that gap earns the right to sit in the middle.

02Thesis

Trust should be computed, not claimed.

Every trust signal on AsapLocal is derived from a verifiable external source and recomputed whenever the underlying facts change.

A provider does not apply for a badge and wait for a human to agree. Their standing is a function of what can actually be checked: a government-ID match, a live Companies House record or reviewed trading documents, an insurance policy with a real expiry date, a trade qualification in a regulated category. Change any input and the standing recalculates.

This is the difference between a directory that lists vetted tradespeople and a platform that maintains them.

The same rule cuts the other way. Because standing comes from checks rather than tenure or ad spend, an established firm cannot buy its way past a newcomer who has earned the same badge.

AI Buddy

A free first opinion, before anyone spends a penny.

The homeowner describes what's going wrong in plain English. AI Buddy decides whether it is safe for an ordinary homeowner to fix, and if it is, replies with a short toolkit and step-by-step plan. If detail is missing it asks one clarifying question rather than guessing.

  • Safe by rule. Gas, mains electrics beyond a like-for-like swap, structural work, roofing and work at height are always routed to a qualified professional — with the reason, and never with a step-by-step.
  • One tap from advice to a pro. The conversation becomes the job post, marked as pre-triaged, so the provider receives a problem that has already been described and narrowed down.
  • Earlier than anyone selling leads. A directory starts at "choose a trade". AI Buddy starts at "something's wrong", and the conversation is kept if the homeowner leaves and comes back.

Provider app

Most of the category starts at Post and stops after Match. AI Buddy and the Studio capture intent before it becomes a search; everything after Match is where the data, the retention and the second revenue line actually live.

05Trust ladder

Four rungs. Every one of them externally sourced.

Tier is never set by hand except at the top, and it is recalculated after every verification event.

Bronze

Registered. Account created and business profile completed. Visible on the marketplace, no trust claims made on their behalf.

Silver

Verified. Identity confirmed against government ID, the business confirmed as genuinely trading, and public-liability cover confirmed as genuine and in date.

Stripe Identity · Companies House + director match (limited companies) or reviewed trading documents (sole traders) · verified PL policy, expiry in future
Gold

Qualified. Everything in Silver, plus a verified trade qualification wherever the category is regulated, plus real portfolio work on file.

Regulated categories carry suggested bodies (e.g. NICEIC · NAPIT · ECA for electrical)
Platinum

Partner. Manual override, reserved for businesses we have worked with directly and stand behind ourselves.

The part competitors structurally cannot copy quickly: insurance is stored with its expiry date, not as an image. A policy that lapses drops the provider's tier automatically and triggers a renewal prompt. There is no "verified once, verified forever" state anywhere in the system.
06Fair start

A first-year trader competes on the same terms as a thirty-year firm.

On most platforms, the work flows to whoever has the biggest budget and the longest review history. We built the lead marketplace so that new and up-and-coming tradespeople compete on their work instead.

  • Same moment, same price. When a job is posted, every eligible provider in that trade and service area is alerted at once. The lead price is set by the size of the job — never by an auction, and never by plan tier.
  • Five seats, not fifty. At most five providers can hold a job, so a newcomer's quote is actually read.
  • Trust earned by checks, not seniority. Nothing on the ladder depends on review count or years trading. A sole trader with ID, trading documents and in-date insurance reaches Silver in their first week — and Silver is what opens gated, high-value categories like Builders & Renovation, which require verified insurance rather than a limited company.
  • Start at £0. A free plan, pay-per-lead from £5, and refunds on bad leads. Pro and Premium are there when the work justifies them, not before.
  • AI that levels the paperwork. Quote templates are drafted from the job details and replies suggested in chat, so a sole trader's first quote reads like an established firm's.
  • Clearer jobs from day one. Posts that start in AI Buddy or the Studio arrive carrying the diagnosis or the design brief, so less of a newcomer's time goes on decoding vague enquiries.
Where the line is drawn: featured placement is a paid position in browse search, and it stays that way. The lead marketplace — where posted jobs are distributed — is not for sale.
07Lead economics

Deliberately scarce, priced to the job, refundable.

The single loudest complaint in this category is lead quality and lead flooding. We designed the marketplace around fixing exactly that.

£5–£20Lead price band
5Max buyers per job
7 daysLead expiry
100–400mPre-purchase blur
  • Price scales with job value. Lead cost is computed from the customer's stated budget, so a £60 job never costs the same to win as a £3,000 one.
  • Hard cap of five. Once five providers hold a job, it closes to the market. The sixth quote never exists, so the five who paid keep a real chance.
  • Four ways to acquire. Monthly plan allowance, prepaid credits, single card purchase, or a free claim — providers pick their own risk posture.
  • Refunds with named reasons. Out of area, duplicate, spam, unresponsive customer, wrong category. An approved refund reverses the exact charge and returns the slot to the market.
  • Address privacy before payment. The map pin is deterministically offset 100–400m until a provider commits, so nobody harvests customer addresses by browsing.

The strategic point: scarcity is a feature we sell to providers. It costs us short-term lead revenue per job and buys the supply-side loyalty that the incumbents have spent a decade burning.

10Wedge

Emergency work first, then everything around it.

Eleven categories are live — cleaning, plumbing, electrical, builders and renovation, gardening, handyman, painting, removals, tutoring, beauty and pet services — but the entry point is narrower than that on purpose.

Emergency jobs are tagged distinctly across the taxonomy: emergency plumbing, leak repair, drain unblocking, emergency electrician, boiler faults. They are the ideal wedge because they are urgent, high-intent, comparatively price-insensitive — and the exact moment a homeowner cares most whether the person arriving is real, insured and qualified.

That is precisely where a computed trust ladder converts best — and where AI Buddy earns its place, answering "is this serious?" and routing the dangerous ones straight to a qualified pro.

Behind the emergency sits the planned work: kitchens, bathrooms, lofts, gardens. The Redesign Studio is how we reach it, turning a household we helped once into a scoped, budgeted renovation job. We win the highest-anxiety job first, then hold the household for the scheduled work that follows.
