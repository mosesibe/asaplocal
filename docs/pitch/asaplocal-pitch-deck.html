Trust,
engineered.

Every marketplace says its tradespeople are vetted. We are the one that can prove it — continuously, in code.

The problem

Both sides distrust the middle.

Homeowner

"Verified" usually means a form was filled in once, years ago. Insurance is collected as a PDF and never checked again.

They carry a risk they were told was handled.

Tradesperson

They buy introductions, not outcomes. One job is resold to as many buyers as the platform can find.

Bad leads are treated as their cost, not the platform's.

The gap

Trust in this category is asserted, never proven.

Whoever closes that gap earns the right to sit in the middle.

Our thesis

Trust should be computed, not claimed.

Every trust signal is derived from a verifiable external source and recomputed whenever the underlying facts change — a government-ID match, a live company record, an insurance policy with a real expiry date, a trade qualification in a regulated category.

A provider cannot type their way to a badge.

The system

Three surfaces, one spine.

Customer app

Post a job with address lookup and AI category matching. Compare quotes, book, pay, sign off, review.

Provider app

A business tool, not a lead inbox — marketplace, calendar, job sheets, staff, portfolio, verification, ROI analytics.

Operations console

Dispatch, verification queue, approvals, refunds, finance, full audit trail. The layer that lets us run jobs.

Coverage

We own the whole job — not the introduction.

Post→Match→Quote→Book→ Deposit→Work log→Sign-off→Review→Payout

Most of the category stops after Match. Everything downstream is where the data, the retention and the second revenue line actually live.

The trust ladder

Four rungs. Every one externally sourced.

Bronze

Registered. Profile complete. No trust claims made on their behalf.

Silver

Verified. Government-ID identity check, company register match, and public-liability cover confirmed genuine and in date.

Gold

Qualified. Silver, plus a verified trade qualification wherever the category is regulated, plus real portfolio work.

Platinum

Partner. Manual override — businesses we have worked with directly and stand behind ourselves.

The part that is hard to copy

Insurance is stored with its expiry date, not as an image.

A policy that lapses drops the provider's tier automatically. There is no "verified once, verified forever" state anywhere in the system.

Retrofitting this means re-verifying an entire existing supply base — and telling a large share of them they just lost their badge.

Why the cap matters

Scarcity is a feature we sell to providers.

It costs us short-term lead revenue per job and buys the supply-side loyalty incumbents have spent a decade burning.

An incumbent with lead-volume targets cannot match it without repricing its entire book.
